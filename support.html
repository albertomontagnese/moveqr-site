MoveQR Support

Find anything you packed.

Email alberto.montagnese@gmail.com for help. Include your iOS version and a short description.

Create and find a box

Tap +, give the box a name and destination room, then enter comma-separated items. Search checks box names, rooms, codes, and every saved item.

Scan a label

Open a box to display its QR label. On a supported physical iPhone, tap the scan button and point the camera at a MoveQR label. Camera scanning is not available in the iOS Simulator.

Delete your data

Delete individual boxes from their detail screens. Deleting the app removes all MoveQR data from the device.
