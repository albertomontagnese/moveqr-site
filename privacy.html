Privacy Policy

Effective September 2026

MoveQR is a local-first app. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data stored on your device

Box names, room names, item lists, handling tags, QR identifiers, and unpacking status are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Camera

Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a QR label. Camera images are processed by Apple's on-device scanner and are not uploaded or retained by us.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, account system, or developer-operated server. Apple may provide aggregate diagnostics if you separately enable Share with App Developers in iOS settings.
